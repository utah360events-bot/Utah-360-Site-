FAQ

Questions we hear before the party.

Straight answers for weddings, company parties, and birthdays in Utah County, Salt Lake County, and Park City. If your date is the real question, send it over.

Where do you serve?

Utah County, Salt Lake County, and Park City. That includes Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, and Salt Lake City.

What kinds of events do you book?

Weddings, corporate events and holiday parties, birthdays, and other private events. Weddings and corporate holiday parties are a focus.

What comes with the 360 booth?

The 360 platform fits up to 5 guests per spin. You also get unlimited HD slow-motion videos, props, a custom overlay, one song, stanchions, LED lights, delivery, setup and takedown, an onsite operator, and videos shared directly to guests by AirDrop or text.

Does the 360 booth come with an attendant?

Yes. An onsite operator runs it the whole time.

How does the digital photo booth work?

It is self-serve. We deliver, set it up, and take it down, and guests run it themselves with no attendant during the event.

Does the digital booth print photos?

No. It is digital only. Guests get unlimited photos and download them by AirDrop or text.

What can I add to the digital booth?

A backdrop, a prop table, custom branding or overlay, and a red carpet experience with a red carpet and stanchions.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. A certificate of insurance is available on request for venues.

How do I book?

Use the inquire or book button on the site, or call (385) 404-9829 or email utah360events@gmail.com with your date and venue.

Packages and both booths are on the services page. City pages live under Areas we serve in the footer.
