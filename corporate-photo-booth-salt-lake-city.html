On brand

Booths built for brands

A company event does not need a generic prop box. It needs a clip people will post with your name still on it. We build a branded overlay for the 360 videos: your logo, a short intro and outro, and the hashtag you actually want used.

Send the brand kit when you inquire. Colors, logo file, and the line you want on screen are enough for us to mock the frame before the event. Guests still get the slow-motion spin. Your team still gets a gallery that looks like it came from the company, not from a rental catalog.

Salt Lake County and Utah County are the service area. Start from the homepage if you are still choosing a date, or go straight to the quote form once the venue is booked.

Pick the room, then the booth

360 video booth vs. digital photo booth

They are different tools. One is a hosted show. The other is a station guests run themselves. Tell us the event and we will recommend one when we reply.

360° video booth

Slow-motion video, up to five guests a spin, an attendant for the whole rental, plus props, a bubble machine, and a red carpet. Best when the booth should be the moment people talk about on Monday.

Digital photo booth

Still photos on a touchscreen. We set the booth up and take it down. There is no attendant during the event, so guests tap, pose, and send photos by AirDrop or text on their own. Backdrop and a props table are add-ons you can ask about.

FAQs

Can every video carry our logo?

Yes. Ask about a branded overlay when you inquire. We can talk through a logo, a short intro and outro, and a hashtag for that event.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Certificate of Insurance available on request.

How many guests can the booth handle per hour?

The 360 platform holds up to five guests per spin, and an attendant keeps the line moving for the whole rental. The digital booth is self-serve, so guests step up on their own after setup. Send your headcount when you inquire and we will recommend how long to book.

Do you serve downtown hotels and the Salt Palace?

Yes. Downtown Salt Lake hotels and the Salt Palace are in Salt Lake County, which is part of our regular service area along with Utah County. Send the hall, the load-in door, and the start time when you inquire.

Can we get every clip after the event?

Yes. You can have every clip after the event, and guests can take theirs during the party by AirDrop, text, or QR code.

How is pricing set?

By hours and add-ons. Inquire with your date and we will send a quote.
