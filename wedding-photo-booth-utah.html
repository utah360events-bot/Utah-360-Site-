Two booths

A 360 video booth or a digital photo booth

Wedding photo booth rental in Utah usually comes down to the moment you want. The 360 video booth fits up to 5 guests per spin, so a bridal party can share one slow-motion video. An onsite operator runs it. Videos are shared directly to guests by AirDrop or text.

The digital photo booth is the self-serve station. We deliver it, set it up, and take it down. Guests run it during the reception, and photos are shared directly to them by AirDrop or text. There are no prints.

Is a photo booth worth it at a wedding?

Many Utah couples add one so the bridal party and friends can be in one slow-motion video, up to 5 guests per spin on the 360 booth. You can also book the digital photo booth for still photos. Send the date and venue for a quote.

How do guests get their video or photos?

On the 360 booth, videos are shared directly to guests by AirDrop or text. On the digital photo booth, guests download photos by AirDrop or text. The digital booth does not make prints.
