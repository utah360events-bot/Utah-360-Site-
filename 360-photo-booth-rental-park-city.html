The mountain weekend

Weddings, retreats, and resort nights

Park City weekends have their own pace. A wedding reception after a canyon ceremony. A company that brought the team up for a retreat. A holiday party while the mountain is in ski season. The 360 booth gives that room a place to gather that is not the bar line.

The camera arm circles the platform in slow motion. An onsite operator from Utah 360 Events runs it the whole time, so the couple or the host is not the one coaching every group. Videos are shared directly to guests by AirDrop or text on the spot. You can have the clips after the event too.

Utah County weddings are on the Provo wedding page. Company parties with a logo are on the Salt Lake corporate page. December and New Year parties are on the holiday party page. Or start from the homepage if you are still choosing a booth.
