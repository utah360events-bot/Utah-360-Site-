Receptions

Weddings and receptions

North county receptions move fast. Dinner, the dances, a room that was a blank hall at three and a full party at six. The 360 booth gives guests a place to go that is not the dessert table. An attendant runs the platform the whole rental. Up to five people per spin. Each group gets a slow-motion video by AirDrop, text, or QR code, and the couple gets every clip after.

Overlay the names and the date. Tell us the colors. We arrive before guests, and setup does not come out of your hours. If the wedding is in Provo proper, the Provo wedding page is the closer brief. This page is for the cities just north of it.

Same booth, three different rooms

Orem, Lehi, and American Fork

Orem

Orem is the UVU city and the State Street reception city. Open houses on campus are a known booking for us. Friday weddings along University Parkway usually have a dance floor we should not block, so the platform goes to the side, in a clear 10×10 ft. If your venue is a hotel ballroom or a neighborhood reception center, send the room name. We have already learned the campus version of this night from the open house above.

Lehi

Lehi weddings often sit near Thanksgiving Point: gardens, museums, and evening events that are not a downtown hotel. The loading is different from a cultural hall. Newer venues along I-15 tend to have a real dock and a strict vendor arrival window. Tell us that window. Company parties in the same zip code should use the corporate page instead of this one. A Lehi reception stays here.

American Fork

American Fork rooms run smaller than a Lehi showpiece. Downtown halls, family parties, and celebrations closer to the mouth of American Fork Canyon than to Provo center. We ask for a floor plan or two photos before we promise the arm will clear the ceiling. Grad nights and neighborhood parties are as common here as a ballroom wedding, and the attendant matters more when the floor is tight.

FAQs

Do you serve all of north Utah County?

Yes. Orem, Lehi, and American Fork sit in Utah County, which is part of our regular service area along with the rest of Utah County and Salt Lake County. Send the city and the venue.

Can you do school or UVU events?

Yes. We have run the 360 booth for a UVU open house in Orem, and we book school, grad, and campus events. Tell us the building when you inquire.

How much space is needed?

The 360 booth needs a clear area roughly 10×10 ft, plus a check on ceiling height. The digital booth is more compact. Confirm the room with us when you inquire.

Can guests share videos instantly?

Yes. On the 360 booth, guests take their clip by AirDrop, text, or QR code during the event, and you can have every clip afterward.
