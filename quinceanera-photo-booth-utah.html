A quinceañera photo booth for the court and the family

A quinceañera photo booth rental in Utah is usually about one moment: her, the court, and the family in a video they can keep. The 360 video booth fits up to 5 guests per spin, so the whole court can share a spin when the group is that size. An onsite operator runs the booth. Videos are shared directly to guests by AirDrop or text.

Ask for a custom overlay with her name, the date, and the colors of the night. After the party you can have the videos as well.

The digital photo booth is the quieter choice. We set it up and take it down. Guests run it, and photos are shared directly to them by AirDrop or text. There are no prints.

FAQs

Can the overlay include her name?

Yes. Tell us her name, the date, and the colors when you inquire. The 360 video can carry a custom overlay.

Can the court fit in one spin?

The 360 booth fits up to 5 guests per spin, so a small court can share one slow-motion video. If the court is larger, the operator helps groups take turns.

Do you also have a digital photo booth?

Yes. The digital photo booth is self-serve. Guests download photos by AirDrop or text. It does not make prints. The 360 booth is the hosted slow-motion video.
