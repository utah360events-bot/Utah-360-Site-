The reception

Why couples in Provo add a 360 booth

A Provo reception already has the long table, the family from out of town, and a dance floor that wakes up after dinner. The 360 booth is where that energy goes between songs. A camera arm circles the platform in slow motion. Up to five guests step on for each spin, and every group walks away with a video of their own.

Couples book it because posed portraits cannot hold the whole room. The booth can. Friends who will not stand still for a photographer will wait in this line, especially once the first clip starts making the rounds. It is built for Utah County weddings: an attendant who keeps the line kind, and a moment people still pull up on their phones the next morning.

If the party is north of Provo, the Orem, Lehi, and American Fork wedding page covers those rooms. If you want still photos instead of slow motion, look at the digital photo booth. Or start from the homepage and tell us the date.

Rooms we know

Provo and Utah County venues we serve

Utah County is home base. If the reception is in the county, send the venue name with your date. We will tell you whether the room can hold the platform.

Reception centers, barns, gardens, canyon venues

Provo halls along Center Street and University Avenue, garden ceremonies that move inside for the party, and barns south of the city all work when there is a clear patch of floor off the buffet. Provo Canyon and Sundance-area venues are a question of access and timing. Inquire with the venue name and we will confirm it before you hold the date. This is Utah County travel, talked through one room at a time.

Space and power needs

Plan for a clear area roughly 10×10 ft so the arm can turn without clipping a chair. We also look at ceiling height. A standard wall outlet is usually enough. Tell us about the room, or send a photo of the corner you have in mind, and we will confirm power and layout when you inquire.

What to tell the venue

Coordinators mostly need three things: when we load in, where the platform sits, and that an attendant stays with it. We arrive early so the head table is not waiting on cables. If the venue has a preferred load-in door, put that in the note with your date.
