How the digital booth works

It is a clean touchscreen on a stand, not a spinning camera arm. A guest taps the screen, a countdown starts, and the booth takes a set of photos. They send the set by AirDrop or text, straight to a phone. No app. No print queue. No one from our crew standing there to press the button.

Someone from Utah 360 Events delivers the booth, sets it up, and comes back to take it down. That is the whole staffing model. During the event the booth is self-serve. Your guests, a parent with a kid, or a volunteer at a check-in table runs it.

Self-serve during the event

Setup and takedown are included. There is no attendant with the digital booth while the party is going. If you want a person on site the entire time, that is the 360 video booth, not this one.

Ask for them by name

Add-ons

The booth itself is the rental. These can be added. Say which ones you want when you inquire and we will confirm they fit the room, then send the quote back the same day.

Backdrop

A backdrop behind the screen so the photos have a finished wall instead of whatever the venue left there. Useful in a gym, a lobby, or a living room with a busy bookshelf.

Curated props table

A props table chosen for the event. Birthday, holiday, or a simple party. We pack to the theme you describe. Guests grab what they want. Nobody has to hand the pieces out.

Backdrop and props bundle

Backdrop and the curated props table together, when you want the station to look complete without adding a host. Name the bundle when you ask for a quote.

Where it earns its keep

Do not book the wrong one

Digital booth vs. the 360 booth

Digital photo booth

Still photos. Self-serve after we set up, and self-serve until we take it down. No attendant during the event. Backdrop and props are add-ons. Best when guests can run it and you want photos, not video.

360° video booth

Slow-motion video. An attendant stays for the whole rental. Up to five guests a spin, with props, a bubble machine, and a red carpet included. Best when the booth should be hosted.

FAQs

How do guests get their photos?

By AirDrop or text. Guests tap the screen, take the photos, and send them to their own phone. There is no app to install and no print to wait on.

Is there an attendant?

The digital booth is self-serve during the event. Someone from Utah 360 Events sets the booth up and comes back to take it down. There is no attendant with the booth while your event is going.

Can I add a backdrop or props?

Yes. Backdrop, a curated props table, or a backdrop and props bundle can be added. Ask when you inquire and we will confirm what fits the room.

Can I upgrade to the 360 booth?

Yes. If you want the attended slow-motion booth instead, say so when you inquire. The Provo wedding page describes that experience, and we can book it for Utah County or Salt Lake County.
