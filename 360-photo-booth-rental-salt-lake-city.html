The spin

What the 360 booth is

A camera on an arm travels around a small platform. The people in the middle stay put, strike a pose, and the clip comes back in slow motion. Up to five guests fit on a spin. It is not a photo strip and it is not a selfie station. It is a short video with your group in the center.

In Salt Lake County that usually means a ballroom, a hall, or a gym that can spare a clear patch of floor. An attendant from Utah 360 Events runs it the entire rental, so you are not the person explaining the platform to every cousin. Sharing is instant: AirDrop, text, or a QR code. You can have the full set of clips after the event.

Company parties with a logo and a venue that wants insurance paperwork are written up on the corporate photo booth page. If you want still photos and no attendant during the party, that is the digital booth. The homepage shows both.

Salt Lake nights

Events we bring it to

Weddings

Receptions in Salt Lake hotels and neighborhood halls. The booth sits off the dance floor, the overlay carries the names, and the attendant keeps the line moving between songs. Send the venue coordinator's load-in time with the date.

Quinceañeras

The court, the family, and a dress that needs room to turn. Slow motion is kind to a big skirt if the platform is clear. Tell us how many people you want in a single spin. Five is the max, and the attendant will help stage it.

Birthdays

Milestone birthdays where the guest list spans kids and grandparents. The attendant helps the smaller guests on and off. Props are picked for the party you described, not pulled from a leftover wedding bin.

Proms

A gym or a hotel ballroom, a tight schedule, and students who already know they want the clip on their phone before the bus leaves. We need the school's rules on arrival, power, and where the arm is allowed to swing. Ask early. Prom weekends stack up in the spring.

Salt Lake County cities

Salt Lake City

Downtown hotels, the Salt Palace when the event is in that building, and house parties in the Avenues or Sugar House. Hotels care about the service elevator and the time we can be on the dock. Put that in the inquiry. A house party cares about ceiling height and whether the platform fits without moving a piano.

Sandy, Draper, and the south valley

Reception centers and quinceañera halls along the bench, plus South Jordan and Riverton parties that are a drive from downtown but still inside the county. These rooms often have a real loading door and a dance floor we should not claim. We place the booth beside it.

Murray, Millcreek, Holladay, Cottonwood Heights

Banquet rooms that fill up for birthdays and school formals. Cottonwood Heights and the east bench run tighter than a west-side gym. Ceiling height is the question, not the drive. A photo of the corner saves a surprised walkthrough.

West Jordan, West Valley, Taylorsville

Community centers and gym floors, where a 10×10 ft pad is usually easy and the crowd is a prom, a birthday, or a family party that invited the whole block. Tell us if the floor is sport court. We will plan the carpet and the cables for that surface.

Salt Lake questions

FAQs

How many people fit on the platform?

Up to five guests per spin. The attendant helps the group get on, get the pose, and get off so the next group can go.

How much space does the 360 booth need?

A clear area roughly 10×10 ft. We also look at ceiling height so the arm can turn. Send photos of the room if you have them.

Do you cover all of Salt Lake County?

Yes. Salt Lake County is a regular service area, together with Utah County. Send the city and the venue when you inquire.

Can the videos include music?

Ask when you inquire. We will confirm how the clip is finished for your event, including the overlay and any audio, before you book.
