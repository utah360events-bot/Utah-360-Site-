What is the difference between the 360 booth and the digital booth?

The 360 video booth fits up to 5 guests per spin and an onsite operator runs it. Videos are shared directly to guests by AirDrop or text. The digital photo booth is self-serve. Guests download photos by AirDrop or text, and it does not make prints.
