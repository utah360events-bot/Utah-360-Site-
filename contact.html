Quick answers

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as possible. Weekends fill up fast, especially in summer and around holidays. A few weeks out is doable, but a few months ahead is better for locking in your date.

How much space does the booth need?

The 360° booth needs roughly a 10×10 ft area. The digital booth is more compact. We'll confirm exact requirements when you book.

Does setup time count against my package hours?

No. We arrive early and have everything ready before your event starts. Your package time is booth-running time only.

Do you travel outside Utah County and Salt Lake County?

Yes, with a travel fee. Just mention your location in the form above and we'll include it in your quote.

Can I customize the props for my theme?

That's part of the process. Props are selected to match every event. Just mention your theme or vibe when you fill out the form.
